what do I do?

I am a Full-stack Software Developer and soon-to-be (Spring 2025) Fordham University graduate, where I have been building a strong foundation in software engineering. My focus is on creating solutions that appear seamless on the surface yet rely on robust, efficient engineering underneath. I am particularly intrigued by the intersection of creativity and technology, developing intuitive user interfaces while designing resilient back-end microservices.


My toolkit includes Java Spring, Python, C/C++, JavaScript, and various modern frameworks. I am equally comfortable spinning up new microservices from scratch, refining CI/CD pipelines for faster and more reliable deployments, and shaping front-end interfaces that truly engage users. Efficiency is a guiding principle in my work, so I consistently look for opportunities to streamline processes and enhance performance.


I stay informed about industry trends and emerging technologies, applying them thoughtfully to produce inclusive software that addresses real-world needs. Software can have a far-reaching impact on everyday life, and my goal is to build solutions that are dependable, user-focused, and forward-thinking.
